# Tente Buyer – guide for buying agents

## 1. Install (once, with signal)
1. Open **Chrome** on your phone. Go to the link Anton sends you.
2. Type your **agent code** (for example MW1) and **PIN**. Tap **Log in**.
3. Tap Chrome's **⋮** menu → **Add to Home screen** / **Install app**.
4. Always open the app from the **mushroom icon** on your home screen.
5. Check your phone's date and time are correct: *Settings → Date & time → Automatic*.

## 2. Record a purchase (works with no signal)
1. Tap **New**.
2. Type the **picker name**. Tap the **species** and the **grade** (A, B, C or Reject).
3. Weigh the crate. Type **Gross kg**. Check **Crate tare kg** (the app remembers it).
4. Check the **price** (the app remembers the last price for each grade).
5. Look at the green box: **Net kg** and **Pay K …**. Count out exactly that amount.
6. Tap **Cash** or **Mobile money**, and where it goes (**Dryer**, **Brine**, …).
7. Tap **Check & save**. Read the summary. Tap **Save**.
8. Show the **receipt** to the picker, or take a screenshot and send it.
9. Write the same purchase in the **paper receipt book** too (for the first weeks).

If the app shows a **⚠ warning** (big weight, unusual price), check again. If it is right, tap **Save anyway**.

## 3. Made a mistake? Void it
You cannot change or delete a saved purchase. That protects you and the cash.
1. Tap **Today** → tap the wrong receipt → **Void this purchase**.
2. Type the reason (for example "wrong weight"). Tap **Void it**.
3. Record the purchase again, correctly.

## 4. New lot
When a batch is closed off (for example sent to the dryer), tap **Start new lot**.

## 5. Syncing
- The black bar at the top shows **how many purchases are waiting** and the **last sync**.
- When you have signal, **open the app**. It sends everything by itself.
- You can also tap **Sync now**.
- **Each evening:** find signal, open the app, and wait for **0 waiting**.
- A **red bar** means: not synced for over 24 hours, or a PIN problem. Find signal, or call Anton.

## 6. No signal for a long time
- Keep working. Everything is saved on the phone.
- Do **not** clear the app's data, uninstall the app, or log out. You would lose the waiting purchases.
- If the phone must go back to the office, or before it breaks: tap **Sync** →
  **Send unsynced by WhatsApp** (or **Share unsynced as CSV file**) and send it to Anton.

## 7. Evening cash check
Tap **Today**. You see the number of purchases, net kg and kwacha paid, split by **Cash** and
**Mobile money**, and by grade. Your cash left over should match.

**Problems?** Call Anton. Never share your PIN.
